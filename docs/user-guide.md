## Purpose

# Introduction

MatterMind is an AI-powered Material Intelligence Platform that assists industries in analyzing materials, predicting compatibility, estimating remaining useful life, and maintaining secure digital material passports.

This guide walks users through the primary features of the platform.

---

# Getting Started

Before using MatterMind, ensure that:

- The application is installed.
- The backend and frontend services are running.
- You have a registered user account.

---

# Login

1. Open the MatterMind application.
2. Navigate to the Login page.
3. Enter your registered email and password.
4. Click **Login**.

After successful authentication, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.

---

# Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of:

- Recent analyses
- Material statistics
- AI recommendations
- Reports
- Recent activity

---

# Uploading Material

To analyze a material:

1. Navigate to **Material Analysis**.
2. Click **Upload Material**.
3. Fill in the required material details.
4. Upload supporting files (if applicable).
5. Click **Analyze**.

---

# Viewing Analysis Results

After processing, the platform displays:

- Compatibility Score
- Risk Assessment
- Remaining Useful Life
- AI Recommendation
- Confidence Score

---

# Reports

Users can:

- View reports
- Download reports
- Review previous analyses
- Compare material results

---

# Blockchain Material Passport

For verified materials, users can view:

- Material ID
- Verification Status
- Blockchain Transaction Hash
- Ownership History
- Timestamp

---

# Search and History

Users can search previous analyses using:

- Material ID
- Material Type
- Date
- Status

---

# User Profile

Users can manage:

- Personal Information
- Password
- Notification Preferences
- Account Settings

---

# Logout

To securely exit the application:

1. Click on your profile.
2. Select **Logout**.

---

- Use accurate material data.
- Keep your account credentials secure.
- Verify analysis results before making decisions.
- Regularly review historical reports.

If analysis does not complete:
