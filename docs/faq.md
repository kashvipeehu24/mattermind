# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Folder

docs/

## Filename

faq.md

## Purpose

This document answers common questions related to the MatterMind project, including its purpose, architecture, technologies, development process, and future scope.

---

# 1. What is MatterMind?

MatterMind is an AI-Powered Material Intelligence Platform designed to analyze materials, generate intelligent insights, estimate remaining useful life, and support sustainable material management through advanced technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Blockchain.

---

# 2. What problem does MatterMind solve?

Many industries struggle with inefficient material management, limited traceability, and difficulty in assessing the condition and reusability of materials.

MatterMind helps organizations make informed decisions by providing intelligent material analysis and secure digital records.

---

# 3. Who can use MatterMind?

The platform is intended for:

- Manufacturing industries
- Recycling facilities
- Material suppliers
- Quality assurance teams
- Research organizations
- Engineers
- Sustainability teams

---

# 4. Why does MatterMind use Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence helps transform machine learning predictions into meaningful recommendations, explanations, and decision support for users.

---

# 5. Why does MatterMind use Machine Learning?

Machine Learning analyzes material properties and predicts:

- Material compatibility
- Remaining useful life
- Risk level
- Prediction confidence

---

# 6. Why is Blockchain included?

Blockchain provides secure and immutable digital material passports, enabling transparency, traceability, and verification throughout the material lifecycle.

---
